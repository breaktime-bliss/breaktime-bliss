About us

About Breaktime Bliss

Welcome to Breaktime Bliss — a small home-based food business born from a love for baking, healthy eating, and homemade goodness.

I’m passionate about creating delicious cookies, desserts, and snacks made with simple, thoughtfully chosen ingredients. My goal is to make treats that feel indulgent while keeping wholesome ingredients at the heart of every recipe.

What started as a passion for experimenting in the kitchen has grown into a little business where everything is made in small batches, with care and attention to detail.

Because we believe that good food can be delicious AND wholesome.
